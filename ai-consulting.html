What we run

The automations we run at District Angels

Everything below runs our own community day to day. It is also the menu we build from for other groups.

Deal intake to pipeline
Founder submissions flow from our intake form into a structured deal database, deduped and tagged on arrival.
First-pass screening
Every inbound deal is scored against our thesis and focus sectors before anyone on the team spends time on it.
Five-day response loop
Founders get an acknowledgment on submit and a tracked nudge, so nobody waits past five days for next steps.
Memo drafting
Diligence notes turn into a structured investment-memo draft that our Fellows and team sharpen.
Market report support
Fellows get research scaffolding and formatting so each market report ships clean under their own name.
Angel matching
Members are matched to deals and to each other by interest, background, and seniority.
Deal room prep
Each monthly session auto-compiles the pitch, memo, and Q&A into one packet for the room.
Member onboarding
New members get a welcome, the right Slack access, and the channels that fit them, with no manual setup.
SPV and commitment tracking
Soft commitments and SPV allocations are tracked from the deal room through to close.
