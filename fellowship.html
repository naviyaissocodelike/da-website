Halo Fellowship

Learn early-stage investing by doing it. Fellows work real deals alongside our angels and publish a market report under their own name.

Apply to the next cohort Ask us a question

Cohort 04 in session · 8 to 10 hours a week · 6 to 10 weeks · No experience required

Who it's for

Students, career-switchers, and professionals who want to understand the startup ecosystem and how early-stage investing actually works. You don't need any prior investing experience.

What you'll do

  1. Learn how deals are evaluated. See how a startup goes from first screen to an investment decision.
  2. Diligence real companies. Work pipeline deals alongside our angels and team.
  3. Write investment memos. Turn diligence notes into a memo the room can act on.
  4. Publish a market report. Pair AI research tools with expert interviews and publish under your own name to grow your brand.
