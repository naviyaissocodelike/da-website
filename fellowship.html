Halo Fellowship

Learn to invest by actually investing

A program for students, career-switchers, and professionals who want to learn about the ecosystem and early-stage investing. Understand how deals are evaluated, practice writing memos, and publish market reports to grow your brand.

  • Every fellow publishes a market report
  • 4 cohorts completed
