Where the capital region backs what's next

District Angels is a DMV-based, volunteer-led angel community. Our mission is to make the DMV a powerhouse of innovation by activating and aligning angel capital. We do this through education, shared infrastructure, and community.

We invest in founders across the US but are excited about angels in or interested in staying close to the DMV.

Philosophy

Angel investing is harder than it should be

We think startup investing should be easy, fun, and rewarding, so we built a way in for anyone who wants to back what's next.

  • Checks start at $2.5K per person. Total investment per deal, pooled into an SPV, runs from $50K to $200K
  • Industry-agnostic, with a special affinity for: Dual-use & defense · Cyber · Biotech · Health tech · Life sciences · Climate · Fintech & crypto
