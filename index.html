Thesis

Angel investing is harder than it should be

New and aspiring angels have nowhere obvious to start, diligence takes time most people don't have, and existing groups can feel closed. Meanwhile the capital region has the talent, the institutions, and some of the highest-income counties in the country, yet almost none of that money reaches the founders here. We think startup investing should be easy, fun, and rewarding, so we built a way in.

  • / Free to join, but curated. We match angels by interest, background, and seniority
  • / Industry-agnostic, with a special affinity for dual-use & defense, cyber, biotech, health tech, climate, and fintech
  • / Checks start at $2.5K per person, pooled into SPVs of $50K to $200K per deal
  • / Open to co-investing alongside other angel groups

For founders

Partners

We're always looking to do more together

We co-host events, put your programs in front of an engaged room of investors and operators, and trade deal flow both ways with clean terms and reciprocity. We don't do any of this alone, so if that sounds like you, we'd love to talk.

The coalition

One region, aligned checkbooks

We're building a coalition of angel groups across the capital region: shared deal flow, co-invested SPVs of $50K to $200K per deal, and joint deal rooms, so a founder who wins one room can win them all.
