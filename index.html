Thesis

Angel investing is harder than it should be

Here's the problem, and what we're doing about it.

  • / New angels have nowhere to start
  • / Diligence takes time and expertise most people don't have
  • / Existing groups can feel closed
  • / The capital region has some of the highest-income counties in the country, yet almost none of that money reaches the founders here

We think startup investing should be easy, fun, and rewarding. So here's our approach:

  • / Free to join, but curated. We match angels by interest, background, and seniority
  • / Industry-agnostic, with a special affinity for dual-use & defense, cyber, biotech, health tech, climate, and fintech
  • / Checks start at $2.5K per person, pooled into SPVs of $50K to $200K per deal
  • / Open to co-investing alongside other angel groups
