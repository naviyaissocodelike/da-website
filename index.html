Where the capital region backs what's next

District Angels is a DMV-based, volunteer-led angel community. Our mission is to make the DMV a powerhouse of innovation by activating and aligning angel capital. We do this through education, shared infrastructure, and events.

We invest in founders across the US but are excited about angels in or interested in staying close to the DMV.

Philosophy

Angel investing in the capital region is harder than it should be. The region has the talent, the institutions, and some of the highest-income counties in the country, yet very little of that money reaches the founders here.

  1. Anyone serious gets a way in. Free to join and curated by interest, background, and seniority. No track record needed.
  2. Small checks, real conviction. Checks start at $2.5K, pooled into SPVs of $50K to $200K per deal.
  3. Diligence is a team sport. Members, fellows, and experts work deals together, and our own AI tooling handles the busywork.
  4. The region wins together. We co-invest alongside other angel groups instead of competing with them.
  5. Sector-agnostic, with depth. Strongest in dual-use & defense, cyber, biotech, health tech, climate, and fintech.
